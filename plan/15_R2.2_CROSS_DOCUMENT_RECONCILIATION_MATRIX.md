## 3. Mandatory `R2.2` & `PKG-M00-02` Technical Audit Corrections Integrated into Blueprint

### 3.1 Fix 1 — `AG-15` Krawczyk / Interval-Newton Existence, MVT Diagonal Exclusion & Validated AABBs
* **Defect Identified in `R2.1.2-R1` / `Checkpoint 13A` & Integration Review:** Naive point-sampled bounding boxes cannot prove non-local offset self-intersection rigorously, and legacy same-segment heuristics skipping tiny boxes by parametric distance (`|t1 - t2| < 1e-4` and `depth > 12`) risk silently dropping genuine tight self-intersections.
* **Mandatory `R2.2` / `PKG-M00-02` Standard (`c1_cert_oracle.py`):**
  1. **Validated Interval AABBs (`validatedIntervalAabbs`):** Each sub-arc of the offset contour $\mathbf{O}(t)$ is enclosed in conservative interval bounds obtained from centered Taylor `mpmath.iv` evaluations serialized outward into IEEE-754 binary64 (falling back to conservative base-curve + normal margin $\mathbf{B}(I) \pm |d|$ if $0 \in Q(I)$ on a coarse seed panel), guaranteeing zero clipping false negatives.
  2. **MVT / Rolle Injectivity Trivial-Diagonal Exclusion:** A same-segment candidate pair $i_1 \times i_2$ near the diagonal is excluded **if and only if** the Mean Value / Rolle Injectivity Criterion holds on the enclosing hull $H = i_1 \cup i_2$:
     $$0 \notin O'_x(H) \quad \text{or} \quad 0 \notin O'_y(H),$$
     proving $\mathbf{O}(t)$ is strictly injective on $H$. No parametric-distance or depth-cutoff heuristic is permitted (`test_pkg_m00_02_ag15_mvt_diagonal_exclusion` verifies tightness at $|t_2^* - t_1^*| = 6 \times 10^{-5} < 10^{-4}$).
  3. **Rigorous Krawczyk Contraction Proof:** On candidate overlapping boxes $X = [t_1] \times [t_2] \subset [0, 1]^2$, compute residual $\mathbf{F}(t_1, t_2) = \mathbf{O}_i(t_1) - \mathbf{O}_j(t_2)$, midpoint Jacobian $J(m)$, preconditioned operator $Y = J(m)^{-1}$, and Krawczyk map:
     $$K(X) = m - Y \mathbf{F}(m) + (I_2 - Y J(X))(X - m).$$
     A unique simple transverse intersection inside $X$ is **certified only if** $K(X) \subseteq \operatorname{int}(X)$ and $\|I_2 - Y J(X)\|_\infty < 1$.
  4. **Fail-Closed `INCONCLUSIVE` / `UNRESOLVED` Handling:** If contraction fails at max depth or a singular base curve retains $0 \in Q(I)$ at `max_depth` (`ZERO_BASE_SPEED_CONTAINED_AT_MAX_DEPTH`), the oracle **must fail closed** with `status = "UNRESOLVED"` / `verification_outcome = "INCONCLUSIVE"` and `failure_category = "INCONCLUSIVE_VERIFICATION"`.

### 3.2 Fix 2 — 5-Category Numerical Assurance Statement & Formal `python-flint` (`arb`) Evaluation

#### 3.2.1 5-Category Numerical Assurance Statement (`PKG-M00-02`)
1. **Pure-Python Non-IEEE-1788 `mpmath.iv` Semantics:** `mpmath.iv` (`1.4.1`) is a pure-Python arbitrary-precision binary interval context and is **NOT formally IEEE 1788-2015 compliant** (lacks formal decoration tags `com/dac/def/trv/ill`). Rigorous behavior is maintained only through verified wrapper invariants (`audit_mpmath_iv_semantics`).
2. **Interval Squaring (`u ** 2`) & Centered Circle Octant Enclosure:** Binary multiplication `u * u` violates dependency across zero-straddling operands (yielding spurious negative lower bounds, e.g. `[-1, 2] * [-1, 2] = [-2, 4]`). All squared speed/norm/displacement terms (`dx**2 + dy**2`, `dt**2`, `x**2 + y**2`) **MUST use `u ** 2`**. Additionally, `_compute_circle_octant_bezier_errors` evaluates $S(t) = x(t)^2 + y(t)^2$ using 2nd-order centered Taylor remainders $S_m + S'_m \delta + \frac{1}{2} S''(t_{\text{iv}})\delta^2$, eliminating linear tangent wrapping and certifying $R=10\text{ cm}$ octant error `< 0.003 cm`.
3. **Exact `Fraction -> iv.mpf` Conversion & Domain Traps:** Raw Python `float` literals (e.g. `0.1`) pre-round non-dyadic rationals before interval entry. All rational parameters enter from `fractions.Fraction` via exact outward division (`iv.mpf(num) / iv.mpf(den)`). Because `mpmath.iv` returns silent `[-inf, +inf]` when dividing by zero-straddling intervals, wrappers explicitly check $0 \in D$ (`q.a <= 0`) and $\inf R < 0$ before downstream use.
4. **Directed Outward IEEE-754 Binary64 Serialization (Zero Shrinking `round()`):** Truncating certification intervals or upper bounds with symmetric Python `round()` can shrink intervals inward by up to $0.5 \times 10^{-k}$ and drop the mathematical truth outside the serialized JSON bound. All certification-bearing fields (`tLower`, `tUpper`, `deltaT`, `lowerChordBoundCm`, `upperControlPolygonBoundCm`, `maxGeometricDeviationBoundCm`, `certifiedIntervalErrorCm`, `minBaseSpeedSquaredInterval`, `minOffsetSpeedFactorInterval`, `validatedIntervalAabbs`, `localSpeedUpperBoundCmPerT`, `edgeAC1CertIntervalCm`, `edgeBC1CertIntervalCm`, `pythonWorstCaseIntervalDeltaCm`) MUST pass through outward IEEE-754 binary64 helpers (`fraction_to_outward_lower_f64`, `fraction_to_outward_upper_f64`, `mpf_to_outward_lower_f64`, `mpf_to_outward_upper_f64`, `iv_to_outward_f64_pair`, verified via `math.nextafter` against `Fraction(f)`). Multi-segment totals accumulate in exact `Fraction` before single outward conversion; `round()` is confined strictly to non-certification `diagnosticFields`, recorded in `serializationAssuranceMetadata`.
5. **Runtime Reconciliation (Per-Curve vs. Full-Population) & Manifest Isolation:** Single-curve degree-12 evaluation benchmarks on Linux `x86_64` (`Python 3.12.16`) measured **`0.9883 ms`** median / **`1.2243 ms`** P95 for `mpmath.iv + Fraction` vs. **`0.0152 ms`** median / **`0.0181 ms`** P95 for C-backed `python-flint 0.9.0` (`~65.0x` per-curve speedup) and **`0.1139 ms`** median / **`0.1259 ms`** P95 for pure-Python `DecimalInterval + Fraction` (all three passing `allThreeCandidatesVerifiedUnder5MsP95`). Across the full **200,000-check qualification suite** (which executes pure-Python `arb_bounds_to_exact_fractions()` and `Fraction` containment checks per operation), full-suite wall-clock runtimes were **`6,281.52 ms`** (`mpmath.iv`) vs. **`2,744.81 ms`** (`python-flint`, **`~2.29x`** speedup) vs. **`3,552.85 ms`** (`DecimalInterval`; total suite `12,605.73 ms`). Wall-clock timings and host kernel strings are strictly isolated into `artifacts/m00/IntervalCandidateBenchmarks.json` and `artifacts/m00/RuntimeBenchmarkObservation.json` and excluded from deterministic manifests (`ReproducibleEnvironmentManifest.json`, `M00_ARTIFACT_HASH_VERIFICATION.json`, and `SchemasLockReport.json` with repo-relative `schemaPath`).
