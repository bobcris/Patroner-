### 4.2 Remaining Technical Limitations & Escalation Boundaries
1. **Adaptive Gauss–Legendre Discrepancy (`Method C1-Est`):** Remains an empirical runtime error estimator (`CONSERVATIVE_ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE`) and cannot provide a mathematical upper bound for curves with high-frequency derivative oscillations between quadrature nodes. Formal certification of arc lengths relies exclusively on `Method C1-Cert` (De Casteljau chord vs. control-polygon convex-hull enclosure in `mpmath.iv`).
2. **Interval Over-Approximation Near Grazing Tangency (`INCONCLUSIVE` State):** On curves where $\min_{t \in [0,1]}(1 + s\,d\,\kappa(t))$ lies within $10^{-30}$ of zero or $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{margin}}$, Bernstein + `mpmath.iv` subdivision may remain `INCONCLUSIVE` even after the 4-step escalation ladder ($k_{\max}=32$, `mp.dps=160`). In such cases, the system fails closed (`Verification_Pending`, `is_production_ready = False`) and requires human patternmaker spline adjustment rather than making an unverified validity or invalidity claim.
3. **3D Cloth Drape Discretization (`Domain 6`):** Finite-element 3D cloth drape and IPC contact equilibrium do not admit closed-form interval enclosures over arbitrary garment meshes; numerical credibility in `M06` is governed by `HIGH_PRECISION_NUMERICAL_REFERENCE` (`EMPIRICAL_ESTIMATE`) against analytical benchmarks B1–B4 (`Dossier 10`) and physical toile trials (`AC-09`), never labeled `CERTIFIED_BOUND`.
