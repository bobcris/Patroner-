## 1. Executive Summary of Blueprint Revision 2.1.1 (`R2.1.1`)

Patroner is an integrated fashion creation, deterministic garment engineering, manufacturing coordination, brand-building, and commerce ecosystem (`v5.16 Ch. 01, p. 5`). Its defensibility lies in the unbroken, evidence-governed continuity of a **Fashion Project** from initial creative inspiration and multimodal AI interpretation (`M04a`) through verified anthropometrics (`M02c`), controlled recipe compilation (`M04b`), deterministic pattern drafting (`M02d`/`M02e`), certified seam/offset geometry (`M02a`/`M02f`), multi-size grading (`M02g`), deterministic marker nesting (`M02h`), material/fit validation (`M03`/`M06`), industrial tech packs (`M05`), qualified workshop sampling and shop-floor execution (`M07`), multi-currency financial reconciliation (`M08`), controlled commercial pilot validation (`M09`), and institutional scale (`M10`).

**Blueprint Revision 2.1.1 (`R2.1.1`)** is a narrowly scoped mathematical, numerical-assurance, migration-boundary, and governance correction to the published `R2.1` baseline (`617cc7f5d9e41b8cd55ed96b64d2363cdb29557b`). It preserves 100% of the approved product vision and `R2.1` structural corrections (`11_BLUEPRINT_R2_TO_R2_1_CHANGELOG.md`) while resolving the seven review findings documented in [`12_BLUEPRINT_R2_1_TO_R2_1_1_CHANGELOG.md`](./12_BLUEPRINT_R2_1_TO_R2_1_1_CHANGELOG.md).

## 2. Core Governing Rules & `R2.1` / `R2.1.1` Refinements

| Area | Governing Rule in Blueprint Revision 2.1.1 (`R2.1.1`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Role of Existing TypeScript Code** | **No mechanical translation.** Every legacy module is audited against governing specifications (`v5.16` & Dossiers 01–16). Test vectors are partitioned into **Class A (Legacy Reference)** and **Class B (Normative)** (`04_MIGRATION_AND_PARITY_POLICY.md`). Known TS defects (`DEF-M00-01`–`DEF-M00-06`) are never replicated in Python. |
| **Five-Concept Numerical Assurance & Triangle Inequality (`Correction A`, `ADR-009`)** | Enforces $\|X_{\mathrm{float64}} - X_{\mathrm{exact}}\| \le \|X_{\mathrm{float64}} - X_{\mathrm{ref}}\| + \|X_{\mathrm{ref}} - X_{\mathrm{exact}}\| = E_{\mathrm{obs}} + B_{\mathrm{ref}} \le B_{\mathrm{cert}} \le \tau_{\mathrm{domain}}$. Never assumes $\|X_{\mathrm{ref}} - X_{\mathrm{exact}}\| = 0$. Separates **(1) $X_{\mathrm{ref}}$**, **(2) $E_{\mathrm{obs}}$**, **(3) $B_{\mathrm{ref}}$**, **(4) $B_{\mathrm{cert}}$**, and **(5) $\tau_{\mathrm{domain}}$**. If $B_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is not rigorously bounded, the check is labeled `HIGH_PRECISION_NUMERICAL_REFERENCE` (`EMPIRICAL_ESTIMATE`), never `CERTIFIED_BOUND`. |
| **`Method C1-Est` Downgrade vs. `Method C1-Cert` Enclosure (`Correction B`, `ADR-009`)** | Downgrades the adaptive Gauss–Legendre quadrature-difference formula $E_{\mathrm{est}} = S_{\mathrm{safety}}\cdot\|I_{\mathrm{GL}(n)} - I_{\mathrm{GL}(2n)}\|$ ($S_{\mathrm{safety}}=16.0$, `Method C1-Est`) to a **`CONSERVATIVE_ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE`** (`EMPIRICAL_ESTIMATE`). Certified arc-length bounds use **`Method C1-Cert`** (De Casteljau chord vs. control-polygon convex-hull interval enclosure $\underline{L}_{\mathrm{chord}} \le L_{\mathrm{exact}} \le \overline{L}_{\mathrm{poly}}$ in `mpmath.iv`). |
| **Six-Category Offset Failure Taxonomy & Sign Convention (`Correction C`, `ADR-009`)** | Independently classifies **(1) `REGULARITY_MARGIN_VIOLATION`**, **(2) `ACTUAL_ZERO_SPEED_CUSP`**, **(3) `TANGENT_REVERSAL`**, **(4) `LOCAL_OFFSET_SELF_INTERSECTION`**, **(5) `GLOBAL_CONTOUR_SELF_INTERSECTION`**, and **(6) `INCONCLUSIVE_VERIFICATION`** under explicit `CARTESIAN_RH_CCW` sign conventions for $1 + s\,d\,\kappa(t)$. |
| **Tri-State Interval Verification (`Correction D`, `ADR-009`)** | Replaces binary pass/fail at subdivision limit $k_{\max}=20$ with **`CERTIFIED_VALID`** ($\underline{F} > 0$), **`CERTIFIED_INVALID`** ($\overline{F} \le 0$ or isolated root), and **`INCONCLUSIVE`** ($\underline{F} \le 0 < \overline{F}$). `INCONCLUSIVE` blocks release promotion (`is_production_ready = False`) without ever being conflated with a confirmed geometric defect. |
| **Strict `M00` vs. `M02` Scope Boundary (`Correction E`)** | Restricts **`M00` (`M00.1`–`M00.10`)** strictly to inventory, defect reproduction, legacy freeze, Class A/B fixtures, and the independent reference-oracle harness (`M00.4` in `services/fashion-engine/tests/oracles/`). All authoritative production geometry (`M02a`) and offset/trimming/validation (`M02f`) belong exclusively to **`M02` (`services/fashion-engine/app/pattern_engine/`)**. |
| **Six-Stage Authorization Gate Matrix (`Correction F`)** | Maps `DEC-01`–`DEC-13` and `ADR-001`–`ADR-009` across **`Gate Auth-1`** (`M00` start — currently `BLOCKED`), **`Gate Auth-2`** (`M01`/`M02` build entry), **`Gate Auth-3`** (`Vertical Slice`), **`Gate Auth-4`** (`Release Level A` pilot entry), **`Gate Auth-5`** (`Release Level B` pilot exit), and **`Gate Auth-6`** (`Release Level C` enterprise scale). |
| **`M04a` vs `M04b` Separation (`Correction 01`)** | **`M04a` (Multimodal Perception & Design Intent)** depends only on `M01` and executes *prior* to deterministic drafting, producing a user-confirmed `GarmentIntentSpecification`. **`M04b` (Controlled Recipe Compilation & Engine Integration)** depends on `M04a`, `M02c`, `M02d`, `M02e`, and `M03` to compile `GarmentRecipe` / `RecipeOperatorAST` / `DraftPlan`. |
| **Unified `M02a`–`M02h` Sub-Workloads & Marker Sequence (`Correction 02`)** | `M02` is decomposed into 8 canonical sub-workloads (`M02a`–`M02h`). `M02h` sits on the critical path to `M05` and the Vertical Slice (`Step 12`). |
| **ISO 4217 Multi-Currency Ledger (`Correction 06`, `ADR-008`)** | Enforces currency-specific `minor_unit_exponent` (`0`, `2`, `3`, `4`), integer minor-unit storage, explicit rounding policies, immutable FX rate snapshots, dual transaction/functional currency balancing, and FX gain/loss accounting. |
| **Dossier 02 & Dossier 04 Domain Preservation (`Correction 07`)** | Retains 100% of the domain requirements of **Dossier 02 v5.0** and **Dossier 04 v5.2** in explicit 6-column reconciliation matrices (`01_SOURCE_REGISTER_AND_REQUIREMENTS.md`). |

## 4. Non-Negotiable Architectural & Governance Invariants

1. **Authoritative System Boundary (`ADR-001`, `Correction E`):** Python (`services/fashion-engine`) owns authoritative fashion-domain computation (`M02` in `app/pattern_engine/`), independent reference oracles (`M00.4` in `tests/oracles/`), AI perception and recipe compilation (`M04a`/`M04b`), canonical artifact generation, and domain workflows. Next.js (`apps/web`) owns user interaction, presentation, navigation, and client-side diagnostic viewport rendering.
2. **Canonical Fashion World Model (`ADR-002`):** Every domain entity has stable identity, immutable append-only revision history (`GarmentRevisionNode`), guarded current-head pointers, and selective dependency invalidation.
3. **Rigorous Five-Concept Numerical & Tri-State Offset Assurance (`ADR-003`, `ADR-009`, `Corrections A–D`):** Internal geometry uses raw IEEE 754 `float64` with zero intermediate `.toFixed()` rounding; certified error bounds enforce $\|X_{\mathrm{float64}} - X_{\mathrm{exact}}\| \le E_{\mathrm{obs}} + B_{\mathrm{ref}} \le B_{\mathrm{cert}} \le \tau_{\mathrm{domain}}$ via `Method C1-Cert` and `Method C2` (while downgrading `Method C1-Est` to `CONSERVATIVE_ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE`); offset curves are verified over the entire continuous interval $t \in [0, 1]$ across all **6 Offset Failure Categories** under **tri-state interval verification** (`CERTIFIED_VALID`, `CERTIFIED_INVALID`, `INCONCLUSIVE`).
4. **Epistemic Discipline (`ADR-004`):** `M04a` separates visible observations from inferred priors and unresolved ambiguities and never emits raw geometry; `M04b` compiles confirmed `GarmentIntentSpecification` into deterministic `M02` recipes.
5. **Material & Simulation Credibility (`ADR-005`, `ADR-006`):** `M03` separates raw physical test observations from calibrated constitutive parameters; `M06` requires analytical solver convergence (B1–B4) before garment evaluation and constrains optimization to bounded recipe parameters with mandatory `M02` re-validation and human approval.
6. **Six-Gate Authorization & `isProductionReady: false` (`10_RELEASE_AND_GOVERNANCE_POLICY.md`, `Correction F`):** `M00` remains `BLOCKED / UNAUTHORIZED` until `Gate Auth-1` is approved. `isProductionReady` remains `false` by default across all engineering builds and can transition to `true` for a specific garment revision only via a cryptographically verified `SignedManufacturingReleasePackage` (`M05` + `M07` physical sample sign-off).
7. **Commercial Neutrality & Multi-Currency Double-Entry Integrity (`ADR-007`, `ADR-008`):** Patroner Platform, Patroner Own-Label, and Managed Brand Studio operate with strict tenant isolation, 8-role `ProductOfferContract` bindings, and an ISO 4217 multi-currency balanced double-entry ledger.
