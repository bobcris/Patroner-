## 1. Executive Summary of Blueprint Revision 2.1.2 (`R2.1.2`)

Patroner is an integrated fashion creation, deterministic garment engineering, manufacturing coordination, brand-building, and commerce ecosystem (`v5.16 Ch. 01, p. 5`). Its defensibility lies in the unbroken, evidence-governed continuity of a **Fashion Project** from initial creative inspiration and multimodal AI interpretation (`M04a`) through verified anthropometrics (`M02c`), controlled recipe compilation (`M04b`), deterministic pattern drafting (`M02d`/`M02e`), certified seam/offset geometry (`M02a`/`M02f`), multi-size grading (`M02g`), deterministic marker nesting (`M02h`), material/fit validation (`M03`/`M06`), industrial tech packs (`M05`), qualified workshop sampling and shop-floor execution (`M07`), multi-currency financial reconciliation (`M08`), controlled commercial pilot validation (`M09`), and institutional scale (`M10`).

**Blueprint Revision 2.1.2 (`R2.1.2`)** is a targeted mathematical, numerical-assurance, and governance-alignment correction to the published `R2.1.1` baseline (`8805140b1e6e7799922348369d7e29881102a75e`). It preserves 100% of the approved product vision, `R2.1` structural corrections (`11_BLUEPRINT_R2_TO_R2_1_CHANGELOG.md`), and `R2.1.1` baseline refinements (`12_BLUEPRINT_R2_1_TO_R2_1_1_CHANGELOG.md`) while resolving the six critical findings documented in [`13_BLUEPRINT_R2_1_1_TO_R2_1_2_CHANGELOG.md`](./13_BLUEPRINT_R2_1_1_TO_R2_1_2_CHANGELOG.md):
1. **`Critical Correction A` — Offset Derivative Sign Error (`ADR-009 §4`):** Corrects the offset tangent derivative under $\mathbf{O}(t) = \mathbf{B}(t) + s\,d\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}_+(t)$ and $\hat{\mathbf{n}}_+'(t) = -\kappa(t)\,\mathbf{B}'(t)$ to $\mathbf{O}'(t) = (1 - s\,d\,\kappa(t))\mathbf{B}'(t)$ and $f(t) = 1 - \frac{s\,d\,C(t)}{Q(t)^{3/2}}$ ($Q(t) > 0$), treats $Q(t) = 0$ separately before dividing by $Q(t)^{3/2}$ or evaluating $P_{\mathrm{exact}}(t) = Q(t)^3 - d^2 C(t)^2$, enforces the concave/evolute sign gate $s\,d\,C(t) > 0$, verifies the CCW circle sanity check, and specifies 14 mandatory adversarial geometry test families.
2. **`Critical Correction B` — Parameter Uncertainty vs. Geometric Output Error (`ADR-009 §2.3`):** Prohibits treating parameter bracket half-width $r_{\mathrm{param}} = \frac{\overline{t} - \underline{t}}{2}$ as a bound on geometric output error $\|X(t^*) - X(t_{\mathrm{mid}})\|$ and mandates `Method B1` (validated derivative bound $M_X(I)\,r_{\mathrm{param}} + \delta_{\mathrm{eval}}$) or `Method B2` (direct interval image enclosure $X(I)$).
3. **`Critical Correction C` — Four-Layer `Method C1-Cert` Assurance & `mpmath.iv` Qualification (`ADR-009 §3`):** Separates Layer 1 (Mathematical theorem: non-strict inequality $L_{\mathrm{chord}} \le L_{\mathrm{exact}} \le L_{\mathrm{control\ polygon}}$), Layer 2 (Algorithmic derivation), Layer 3 (8-point `IntervalLibraryQualificationReport` against `fractions.Fraction`), and Layer 4 (Empirical verification), and removes absolute unconditioned mathematical-certification claims for library outputs.
4. **`Critical Correction D` — `DEC-13` / `Gate Auth-1` Circular Dependency Resolution (`07`, `10`):** Splits `DEC-13` into **`DEC-13 Stage 1` (`Gate Auth-1`: Oracle Library Selection via static review, requiring zero `M00` execution)** and **`DEC-13 Stage 2` (`Gate Auth-2`: Production Adoption & `< 5 ms` Performance Gate after `M00.4` executes)**, with an optional pre-`M00` spike (`SPIKE-DEC13-01`) if requested by the Owner before `Gate Auth-1`.
5. **`Critical Correction E` — Gate Authority Consistency (`05`, `06`, `07`, `10`):** Reconciles all `DEC-01`–`DEC-13` and `ADR-001`–`ADR-009` gate mappings against `07_RISK_AND_OPEN_DECISIONS_REGISTER.md` as the single source of truth.
6. **`Critical Correction F` — Five-Dimensional Oracle Independence (`04`, `ADR-009 §6`):** Explicitly distinguishes algorithmic, source-code, arithmetic/library (`fractions.Fraction` cross-check of `mpmath.iv`), input/fixture, and reviewer/verification independence.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Architectural & Governance Invariants

1. **Authoritative System Boundary (`ADR-001`, `Section 9`):** Python (`services/fashion-engine`) owns authoritative fashion-domain computation (`M02` in `app/pattern_engine/`), independent reference oracles (`M00.4` in `tests/oracles/`), AI perception and recipe compilation (`M04a`/`M04b`), canonical artifact generation, and domain workflows. Next.js (`apps/web`) owns user interaction, presentation, navigation, and client-side diagnostic viewport rendering.
2. **Canonical Fashion World Model (`ADR-002`):** Every domain entity has stable identity, immutable append-only revision history (`GarmentRevisionNode`), guarded current-head pointers, and selective dependency invalidation.
3. **Rigorous Five-Concept Numerical, Parameter-to-Output & Tri-State Offset Assurance (`ADR-003`, `ADR-009`, `Critical Corrections A–C, F`):** Internal geometry uses raw IEEE 754 `float64` with zero intermediate `.toFixed()` rounding; certified error bounds enforce $\|X_{\mathrm{float64}} - X_{\mathrm{exact}}\| \le E_{\mathrm{obs}} + B_{\mathrm{ref}}(X) \le B_{\mathrm{cert}}(X) \le \tau_{\mathrm{domain}}(X)$ with mandatory `Method B1/B2` parameter-to-output propagation and 4-layer `Method C1-Cert` / `Method C2` enclosures; offset curves use the corrected derivative $\mathbf{O}'(t) = (1 - s\,d\,\kappa(t))\mathbf{B}'(t)$, separate $Q(t)=0$ check, and $s\,d\,C(t)>0$ sign gate across all **6 Offset Failure Categories** and **14 adversarial geometry test families** under **tri-state interval verification** (`CERTIFIED_VALID`, `CERTIFIED_INVALID`, `INCONCLUSIVE`).
4. **Epistemic Discipline (`ADR-004`):** `M04a` separates visible observations from inferred priors and unresolved ambiguities and never emits raw geometry; `M04b` compiles confirmed `GarmentIntentSpecification` into deterministic `M02` recipes.
5. **Material & Simulation Credibility (`ADR-005`, `ADR-006`):** `M03` separates raw physical test observations from calibrated constitutive parameters; `M06` requires analytical solver convergence (B1–B4) before garment evaluation and constrains optimization to bounded recipe parameters with mandatory `M02` re-validation and human approval.
6. **Six-Gate Authorization & `isProductionReady: false` (`10_RELEASE_AND_GOVERNANCE_POLICY.md`, `Critical Corrections D & E`):** **`R2.1.2 publication does not authorize M00. M00 remains blocked pending explicit owner approval.`** `isProductionReady` remains `false` by default across all engineering builds and can transition to `true` for a specific garment revision only via a cryptographically verified `SignedManufacturingReleasePackage` (`M05` + `M07` physical sample sign-off).
7. **Commercial Neutrality & Multi-Currency Double-Entry Integrity (`ADR-007`, `ADR-008`):** Patroner Platform, Patroner Own-Label, and Managed Brand Studio operate with strict tenant isolation, 8-role `ProductOfferContract` bindings, and an ISO 4217 multi-currency balanced double-entry ledger.
