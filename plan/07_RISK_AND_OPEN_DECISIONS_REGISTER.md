## 1. Purpose and Governance Principle

This register tracks the **Top Engineering & Commercial Risks (`RISK-01` through `RISK-12`)** and the **13 Binding Owner Decisions (`DEC-01` through `DEC-13`)** governing Patroner's execution from `M00` through `M11`.

### 1.1 Resolution of Decision Circularity (`R2.1.2-R1` 4-Stage Governance Lifecycle)
Earlier planning revisions contained a governance contradiction regarding `DEC-13` (Certified Interval & Polynomial Root-Isolation Library): `Gate Auth-1` required final sign-off on `DEC-13` before `M00` could start, while `M00.4` was simultaneously tasked with running the qualification benchmark needed to make that decision, or conversely treated `mpmath.iv` as already qualified before `M00.4` ran.

In `R2.1.2-R1` (`ADR-009 §7.2`), this is resolved via a strict **4-Decision / 4-Stage Lifecycle**:
1. **Decision 1 — Candidate Selection (`DEC-13 Stage 1` at `Gate Auth-1` — APPROVED 2026-11-16):** Before `M00` starts, the Project Owner approved the ordered candidate stack to be evaluated in `M00.4`:
   * **Primary Candidate:** `mpmath.iv` (`53-bit` fast interval screen + `113-bit` quad-precision certification tier) + Python `fractions.Fraction` (exact rational $\mathbb{Q}$ Bernstein-basis sign evaluation).
   * **Secondary Candidate:** `python-flint` (`arb` C-library midpoint-radius ball arithmetic bindings).
   * **Tertiary Fallback:** Pure-Python `fractions.Fraction` + `decimal.Decimal` (`prec=50`) with explicit `ROUND_FLOOR` / `ROUND_CEILING` interval wrapper.
2. **Decision 2 — Library Qualification (`IntervalLibraryQualificationReport` in `M00.4`):** During `M00.4` execution, engineering runs the qualification suite on the target Python container to verify outward rounding, rigorous polynomial enclosure, absence of false negatives on grazing tangencies (`Category 3`), and runtime budget (`<= 50 ms`).
3. **Decision 3 — Oracle Implementation Verification (`M00.4` & `M00.10`):** Engineering builds the `C1-Cert` reference oracle using the qualified library and verifies 100% classification accuracy across all 6 offset categories (`Category 1`, `2a`, `2b`, `2c`, `3`, `4`) and all **17 Adversarial Geometry Test Families (`AG-01`–`AG-17`)**, including `AG-11` ($\mathbf{B}(t) = (t^3, 0)$ removable parameterization degeneracy).
4. **Decision 4 — Production Adoption Sign-Off (`DEC-13 Stage 2` at `Gate Auth-2`):** At `Gate Auth-2` (after `M00.10` completes and before `M02` starts), the Project Owner reviews the completed `IntervalLibraryQualificationReport` and `M00.10` audit bundle to authorize the production interval library for `M02`.

> **Explicit Non-Bypass Rule for `SPIKE-DEC13-01`:** Any optional pre-`M00` exploratory spike (`SPIKE-DEC13-01`) is strictly informational and **cannot** bypass `DEC-13 Stage 1` (`Gate Auth-1`), the mandatory `IntervalLibraryQualificationReport` in `M00.4`, the 17-family verification in `M00.4`/`M00.10`, or `DEC-13 Stage 2` (`Gate Auth-2`).
