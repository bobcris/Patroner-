## 3. Explicit Decision-to-Gate Authorization Matrix (`Correction F`)

To ensure proportionate governance where foundational mathematical/migration decisions gate `M00` (`Gate Auth-1`), core engineering decisions gate their respective `M01`–`M06` workloads (`Gate Auth-2`/`Gate Auth-3`), and commercial/tax/jurisdictional decisions gate `M08` completion and `M09` pilot launch (`Gate Auth-4`) without artificially blocking early geometry or world-model work, the following **8-column Decision-to-Gate Matrix** governs all `ADR-001`–`ADR-009` and `DEC-01`–`DEC-13` items across the six program authorization gates:

### 3.1 Summary of the Six Program Authorization Gates
1. **`Gate Auth-1` — Authorization to Begin `M00` (Migration Preparation & Reference Oracles):**
   - **Current Status:** **`BLOCKED / UNAUTHORIZED`** (`Blueprint R2.1.1` is a planning baseline only; zero code execution is authorized until `ADR-009`, `DEC-01`, `DEC-13`, and explicit Project-Owner commencement authorization are granted).
2. **`Gate Auth-2` — Authorization to Begin Authoritative `M01` / `M02` Implementation:**
   - Requires `M00.10` Exit Audit sign-off plus approval of the specific engineering decision governing each target sub-workload (`DEC-04`/`DEC-05` for `M01`, `DEC-11` for `M02b`/`M02g`, `DEC-06` for `M02h`, `DEC-03` for `M03`, `DEC-09` for `M05.5`, `DEC-02` for `M06.2`).
3. **`Gate Auth-3` — Internal Technical Acceptance (`Vertical Slice Gate`):**
   - Requires 100% completion of the 14-step Vertical Slice (`plan/08_VERTICAL_SLICE_DEFINITION.md`) and closure of `DEC-01`–`DEC-06`, `DEC-09`, `DEC-11`, `DEC-13`, and `ADR-001`–`ADR-006`, `ADR-009`.
4. **`Gate Auth-4` — Controlled Commercial Pilot Entry (`Release Level A Gate`):**
   - Requires `Gate Auth-3` + `M07` ($\ge 2$ qualified workshops & signed physical first-article sample approvals) + `M08` completion + formal sign-off on `ADR-007`, `ADR-008`, `DEC-07`, `DEC-08`, `DEC-10`, and `DEC-12` (legal/tax CPA sign-off).
5. **`Gate Auth-5` — Commercial Pilot Acceptance (`Release Level B Gate`):**
   - Requires measured empirical completion of the `M09` pilot ($\le 50$ real physical units, $\ge 95\%$ first-pass POM QC yield, bounded fit return rates, and $0$ minor-unit cash-to-ledger reconciliation variance).
6. **`Gate Auth-6` — Enterprise-Scale Release (`Release Level C Gate`):**
   - Requires completion of `M10` (`M10.1`–`M10.5`: repeatable Brand Studio waterfalls, BYOK KMS, automated biometric crypto-shredding, enterprise partner APIs, and multi-region DR drills).
