## 1. Authoritative Source Precedence Hierarchy

When reconciling requirements across documents or code, the following strict precedence order governs (`Blueprint Revision 2 §2.2`):

1. **Level 1 — Current Canonical Strategic & Architecture Master:** `Patroner_Living_Project_Monograph_v5.16_Consolidated_Master.pdf` (05 November 2026, Chapters 01–20 & Appendices A–F).
2. **Level 2 — Formal Architecture Corrections & Approved Revisions (interpreted together with v5.16):**
   - `Patroner_Architecture_Correction_Addendum_v4.1.pdf` (Python authoritative computational/AI system; Next.js user-facing platform; modular monolith first; typed versioned contracts).
   - `Patroner_Architecture_Revision_v4.5.pdf` (Fit prediction & bounded pattern optimization loop; independent validation; visual realism as diagnostic interface rather than primary success criterion).
   - `Patroner_Monograph_v5.15_Commercial_Ecosystem_Update.pdf` (Integrated into v5.16 Chapters 01–02, 08, 12–13, Appendices C–D).
3. **Level 3 — Specialist Research Dossiers (Dossiers 01–16, v4.9–v5.14):** Detailed domain formulas, schemas, benchmarks, and security controls referenced by v5.16.
4. **Level 4 — Approved Engineering Decisions & Independently Verified Milestone Records:** Explicitly audited mathematical/cryptographic guarantees (e.g., Phase E3 independent audit findings).
5. **Level 5 — Current Repository Implementation (`e9d99cb4afc54057850f920dc909b23ebde86f1f`):** Evidence of implemented algorithms and test fixtures; never overrides Levels 1–4.
6. **Level 6 — Live Product Behavior (`https://patroner.ai.studio/`):** Evidence of currently deployed static frontend (`07 November 2026`); never overrides Levels 1–5.
7. **Level 7 — New Recommendations:** Clearly marked as `PROPOSED` in ADRs and workload specifications requiring human architectural approval.
