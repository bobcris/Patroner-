## 1. System-of-Systems Architecture Overview

Patroner is architected as a **two-application modular system** (`Addendum v4.1`, `v5.16 Ch. 10, p. 17–18`):

1. **Authoritative Computational & AI System (`services/fashion-engine` — Python 3.12+ / FastAPI):**
   - Owns the canonical **Fashion World Model** (`Dossier 01`, `M01`), identity/tenant/resource policy enforcement (`Dossier 16`, `M01`), workflow orchestration and `EnginePlan` execution (`Dossier 02`, `M01`), multimodal AI perception and design intent (`Dossier 07`, `M04a`), controlled recipe compilation (`Dossier 08`, `M04b`), deterministic geometry/topology/anthropometry/blocks/operators/seams/grading/markers (`Dossiers 03, 08, 13`, `M02a`–`M02h`), material mechanics and ease (`Dossiers 04 & 09`, `M03`), 3D body/avatar representation, cloth simulation, fit evaluation, and bounded Pareto optimization (`Revision v4.5`, `Dossiers 05, 10, 11, 12`, `M06`), production documentation and shop-floor traceability (`Dossiers 14 & 15`, `M05`, `M07`), and the commercial offer and ISO 4217 multi-currency double-entry financial transaction engine (`v5.16 Ch. 12`, `M08`).
2. **User-Facing Experience Platform (`apps/web` — Next.js 15 App Router):**
   - Owns the Consumer Fashion Studio, Professional Pattern/Engineering Workspace, Brand Studio, Curated Capsule Storefront, Workshop Portal, and Admin/Finance surfaces (`v5.16 Ch. 10, p. 17`).
   - Renders 2D SVG CAD geometry, 3D diagnostic `glTF` viewports, measurement intake forms, epistemic disambiguation cards, diff/lineage graphs, and production tech packs exclusively from versioned DTOs (`packages/contracts`) returned by `services/fashion-engine`.
   - Performs local viewport interaction calculations (zoom, pan, hover hit-testing, client-side form syntax pre-checks) **without ever acting as an authority for pattern geometry, grading, marker nesting, validation, fit qualification, authorization, or production approval**.

## 2. Nine-Layer Control Boundary Matrix (`v5.16 Ch. 10, p. 17`)

| Layer | Owning Runtime | Primary Responsibilities | Non-Negotiable Control Boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Experience Layer** | **Next.js (`apps/web`)** | Consumer studio, professional CAD workspace, Brand Studio, storefront, workshop portal, admin views. | UI never decides authorization, technical validity, fit qualification, or `isProductionReady`. |
| **2. Identity, Tenant & Policy** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/iam`)** + Next.js Session BFF | Principals (`Person`), `Organizations`, `Tenants`, `Memberships`, RBAC+ABAC grants, workshop time-bounded grants, consent, audit logs. | Deny by default; enforce object-level and property-level access server-side on every request and 3-point async job check. |
| **3. Canonical World Model** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/world_model`)** + PostgreSQL | `FashionProject`, `BrandProject`, `GarmentRevisionNode` DAG, current heads, dependency invalidation, provenance manifests. | Single source of record; append-only immutable revisions; stale descendants automatically flagged and blocked from reuse. |
| **4. Multimodal AI & Intent (`M04a` / `M04b`)** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/ai_interpretation`)** | **`M04a`**: Reference image & brief interpretation, visible vs. inferred vs. ambiguous partitioning, candidate `GarmentIntentSpecification`.<br>**`M04b`**: Confirmed intent → `GarmentRecipe` / `RecipeOperatorAST` / `DraftPlan`. | `M04a` outputs are strictly candidate proposals requiring human disambiguation; `M04a` has zero dependency on `M02d`/`M02e` drafting; `M04b` compiles confirmed intent into deterministic `M02` plans. |
| **5. Deterministic Engines (`M02a`–`M02h`, `M03`, `M05`)** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/{pattern_engine,materials,production}`)** | `M02a` Geometry, `M02b` Topology/Assembly, `M02c` Anthropometrics, `M02d` Blocks, `M02e` Style Operators, `M02f` Certified Offsets/Notches, `M02g` Grading, `M02h` Marker Nesting, `M03` Material Mechanics, `M05` Tech Packs. | Full IEEE 754 `float64` internal precision, 5-concept certified interval/Bernstein error enclosures (`Method C1-Cert`, `Method C2`, `Method C3`; `Method C1-Est` downgraded to `CONSERVATIVE_ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE` per `ADR-009`), tri-state complete-interval $t \in [0, 1]$ 6-category offset verification, and deterministic reproducibility without AI. |
| **6. Simulation, Fit & Optimization (`M06`)** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/simulation_and_fit`)** | Parametric body vs. 3D scan representation, orthotropic cloth/contact solver, regional fit evaluation, bounded Pareto optimization. | Solver must pass analytical benchmarks B1–B4 (`AC-08`); non-convergence fails closed; optimizer obeys hard recipe parameter bounds and requires independent `M02` re-validation and human approval. |
| **7. Independent Evaluation & Evidence** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/tests/oracles` & verifiers)** | Independent `mpmath` / `mpmath.iv` geometry, Bernstein/interval 6-category offset, cross-size grading, marker non-overlap, simulation benchmark, physical sample, and ledger verifiers (`M00.4` strictly isolated from `M02` production modules per `Correction E`). | Strictly separated from generators/optimizers; enforces the **5-concept numerical assurance model** (`Correction A`), tri-state verification (`CERTIFIED_VALID`, `CERTIFIED_INVALID`, `INCONCLUSIVE` per `Correction D`), and 6-gate release framework (`Gate Auth-1`–`Auth-6`). |
| **8. Orchestration & APIs (`M01.4`)** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/orchestration`)** | Intake validation, `EnginePlan` execution, durable job state machine, idempotency, bounded retries, cancellation, artifact publication. | Re-verifies principal & tenant permissions at enqueue, worker start, and before output commit (`DOS-02-R6`). |
| **9. Commerce, Operations & Ledger (`M07`, `M08`, `M09`, `M10`)** | **Python (`services/fashion-engine/app/{manufacturing_execution,commerce_and_finance}`)** | Maker qualification, quotes, physical sample approvals, `ProductOfferContract`, work orders, inspections, ISO 4217 multi-currency double-entry ledger. | Explicit 8-role offer responsibility; Patroner Own Label isolated as a separate tenant/P&L; currency-specific `minor_unit_exponent` and dual transaction/functional currency balancing. |
